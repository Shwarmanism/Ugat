Project Background

                
The modern landscape of Natural Language Processing (NLP) remains heavily skewed toward high-resource
                    languages like English. Philippine languages, including major dialects such as Tagalog, Cebuano, and
                    Ilocano, distinctly exemplify the challenge of processing low-resource languages with rich,
                    agglutinative morphology.

                
Ugat is an intelligent system specifically designed to accurately detect and
                    transform inflected or derived words across major Philippine dialects into their fundamental root or
                    base forms. By providing a dedicated solution for morphological processing, Ugat aims to bridge the
                    gap and enhance NLP performance for these underserved languages.

            


            

                
The NLP Problem We Address

                
Philippine languages are characterized by complex word variations systematically derived from a
                    single root through prefixes, infixes, suffixes, and reduplication. For example, the Tagalog root
                    word basa (read) can transform into:

                
    
                    
  • nagbasa — read (past tense)
    • 
                    
  • bumabasa — reading (present tense)
    • 
                    
  • babasahin — will be read (future tense)
    • 
                

                
This morphological complexity generates vast vocabulary permutations,
                    making it difficult for standard NLP systems to process them efficiently.
